### About Me:
I'm a 4th-year student at Kyoto University and the CTO of NoCode Solutions, Inc., a Kyoto-based startup that helps companies put AI to work, from planning all the way to production.

At NoCode Solutions, I build LLM-powered business systems for our clients: turning PDFs and business forms into structured data, connecting that data to RAG, and building an AI-native CRM. I own the architecture and cloud infrastructure on Azure, Google Cloud, and AWS, and as CTO I also lead engineering management and hiring.

Alongside that, I work as an engineer at Treee, Inc. on AI that reads and structures 2D CAD drawings. Previously, I was a software engineer intern at Money Forward, where I worked on both the backend (API development, database design) and the frontend (React / Next.js) of a cloud accounting product.

My roots are in competitive Othello, where I once ranked 5th in Japan. Today I research game AI in my lab and am building an Othello AI to climb the CodinGame leaderboard. I also love hackathons: I've joined 9+ of them in 2026 and won awards at several.

Recently, I've become fascinated with Physical AI: taking AI beyond the screen and into robots that perceive and act in the real world.
